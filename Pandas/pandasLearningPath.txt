PANDAS CHEATSHEET - CATEGORIZED
===============================

1) DATA LOADING & INSPECTION
----------------------------
a) Load
   pd.read_csv()
   pd.read_excel()
b) Preview
   df.head()
   df.tail()
   df.sample()
c) Structure
   df.shape
   df.columns
   df.dtypes
   df.info()
   df.memory_usage()
d) Quick look at values
   df.describe()
   df.nunique()
   df["column"].unique()
   df["column"].value_counts()


2) SELECTING & FILTERING
------------------------
a) Columns
   df["column"]
   df[["col1", "col2"]]
   df.filter()
   df.select_dtypes()
b) Rows
   df.loc[]
   df.iloc[]
   df.query()
c) Conditional
   df.where()
   df.mask()
   df["column"].isin()
   df["column"].between(a, b)


3) DATA CLEANING
----------------
a) Missing values
   df.isnull()
   df.isna()
   df.notnull()
   df.notna()
   df.isnull().sum()
   df.dropna()
   df.fillna()
   df.ffill()
   df.bfill()
   df.interpolate()
b) Duplicates
   df.duplicated()
   df.duplicated().sum()
   df.drop_duplicates()
   df.drop_duplicates(subset=[])
c) Data types
   df.astype()
   pd.to_numeric()
   pd.to_datetime()
   pd.to_timedelta()
   df.convert_dtypes()
d) Strings
   df["column"].str.strip()
   df["column"].str.lower()
   df["column"].str.upper()
   df["column"].str.title()
   df["column"].str.capitalize()
   df["column"].str.replace()
   df["column"].str.split()
   df["column"].str.contains()
   df["column"].str.startswith()
   df["column"].str.endswith()
   df["column"].str.len()
   df["column"].str.extract()
   df["column"].str.fullmatch()
e) Replace & standardize
   df.replace()
   df["column"].map()
   df.rename()
   df.rename_axis()
   df.set_axis()
f) Outliers & invalid values
   df.clip()
   df.quantile()
   df.abs()
   df.nlargest()
   df.nsmallest()
   df.mean() / df.median() / df.std()   (used for thresholds)


4) TRANSFORMATION & FEATURE ENGINEERING
---------------------------------------
a) Apply functions
   df.apply()
   df.map()
   df.transform()
   df.pipe()
   df.eval()
   df.assign()
   (Note: applymap() is deprecated; use DataFrame.map())
b) Binning
   pd.cut()
   pd.qcut()
c) Categorical encoding
   pd.get_dummies()
   df.astype("category")
   df["column"].cat.codes
   df["column"].cat.categories


5) SORTING & INDEXING
---------------------
a) Sort
   df.sort_values()
   df.sort_index()
b) Index control
   df.set_index()
   df.reset_index()
   df.reindex()


6) RESHAPING DATA STRUCTURE
---------------------------
a) Add or remove
   df.drop()
   df.drop(columns=[])
   df.pop()
   df.insert()
b) Reshape
   df.T
   df.melt()
   df.pivot()
   df.pivot_table()
   df.stack()
   df.unstack()
   df.explode()


7) COMBINING DATASETS
---------------------
a) Stack
   pd.concat()
b) Join on keys
   pd.merge()
   df.merge()
   df.join()


8) GROUPING & AGGREGATION
-------------------------
a) Group
   df.groupby()
b) Aggregate
   df.agg()
   df.aggregate()
   df.transform()
c) Summary functions
   df.count()
   df.size
   df.sum()
   df.mean()
   df.median()
   df.min()
   df.max()
   df.std()
   df.var()


9) DATE & TIME PROCESSING
-------------------------
a) Extract parts
   df["date"].dt.year
   df["date"].dt.month
   df["date"].dt.day
   df["date"].dt.quarter
   df["date"].dt.dayofweek
   df["date"].dt.day_name()
b) Format & convert
   df["date"].dt.strftime()
   df["date"].dt.to_period()


10) STATISTICS & CORRELATION
----------------------------
a) Relationships
   df.corr()
   df.cov()
   df.corrwith()
b) Distribution & change
   df.mode()
   df.rank()
   df.pct_change()
   df.diff()
   df.value_counts()


11) EXPORT & FINAL VALIDATION
-----------------------------
a) Save
   df.to_csv()
   df.to_excel()
   df.to_json()
   df.to_pickle()
b) Validate
   df.shape
   df.info()
   df.dtypes
   df.isnull().sum()
   df.duplicated().sum()
   df.describe()


NOTES
=====
- read_csv is pd.read_csv(), not df.read_csv().
- between() and isin() are Series methods: df["col"].between(a, b).
- size is an attribute: df.size, or groupby(...).size().
- Replace "column", "col1", "col2", "date" with real column names.
- Choose cleaning strategies based on what the data means; don't
  automatically delete every outlier or fill every missing value.
